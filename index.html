My work sits where LLMs, APIs and n8n meet: agents that read messages, make decisions and move data without supervision. I optimize for boring. A good automation is one nobody has to think about after launch week.

(03) Case studies
Lead generation, local services

Five hundred leads a week, nobody searching.

The problem

A client's sales team was spending about 15 hours a week finding prospects on Google Maps and copying names and numbers into a spreadsheet. Typos, duplicates, and no real qualification.

What I built

  • Apify actor scrapes Maps listings by niche & geography on a schedule
  • GPT-4o qualifies each lead against the ICP and rejects the noise
  • n8n dedupes, formats and syncs the clean list to Google Sheets
  • Slack ping when a batch of hot leads lands
500+qualified leads / week
15hmanual research eliminated weekly
0copy-paste errors since launch
Conversational AI, e-commerce

Every order taken, start to finish.

The problem

A Facebook shop I worked with was losing orders overnight. Customers messaged at 2 AM, got answered at 10 AM, and bought from whoever replied first. Every order meant manual back and forth over product, size, address and payment.

What I built

  • Meta Graph API webhooks pipe every message into n8n in real time
  • GPT-4o holds the conversation, answers questions and collects order details
  • Orders validated and written to Google Sheets with full context
  • Human handoff only on refunds and edge cases
<1minaverage response time, 24/7
